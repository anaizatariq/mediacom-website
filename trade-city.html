MEDIACOM TRADE CITY

The Benchmark of Commercial
Excellence.

Developed in 2004, Mediacom Trade City is a flagship multi-storey commercial complex situated in the heart of Kohinoor Town, Faisalabad. The modern facility integrates a spacious shopping mall featuring national and multinational retail brands, dedicated food courts, and an expansive underground valet parking system.

It also houses executive corporate offices, business centers, and luxury apartments. Supported by uninterrupted power backups, robust security systems, and high-speed elevators, Trade City sets the standard for mixed-use commercial real estate in the region.
